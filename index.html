Booking policy

Read the booking policy

Deposit and booking

  • A non-refundable deposit of R100 is required to secure your booking. It goes toward your total cost.
  • After you book, send the deposit and your booking is confirmed once Thato receives it.

Cancellations and rescheduling

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice means you forfeit your deposit.
  • No-shows are charged the full service fee and may lose online booking privileges.

Arriving late

  • There is a 15 minute grace period.
  • After that, your service may be shortened or rescheduled so other clients aren't delayed. The full fee still applies.

Health and safety

  • We cannot work on nails or skin with fungal infections, open wounds, severe inflammation or contagious conditions. We may decline and reschedule you once treated.
  • Please tell Thato about any allergies to acrylics, gels, acetone or skincare ingredients before your service starts.

Nail art, repairs and removals

  • Custom nail art takes extra time. Please mention it when you book.
  • If a nail chips or lifts within 3 days of your appointment, contact us for a free repair.
  • We do not work over sets done elsewhere. Please ask about a full removal with your new set.

Children and guests

  • Extra guests and children are by prior arrangement only, to keep the space safe and relaxing.
